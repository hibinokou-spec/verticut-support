VertiCut
日本語

Turn landscape footage into vertical Shorts.

VertiCut is an iPhone app that cuts a 9:16 vertical video out of footage shot in landscape (16:9), following your subject automatically as it crops. Where the automatic tracking misses, you can fix it by hand. Everything is processed on your iPhone; your footage never leaves the device.

What it does

  • Tap the person to follow on the first frame, and the whole clip is tracked automatically.
  • To change what is in frame, add a ◆ (keyframe) on the timeline and drag the frame.
  • Switch to a different person partway through, or hold the framing still over a stretch.
  • Save the exported vertical video to Photos.

Questions

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. iPad is not supported.

How do I restore Pro?

On the home screen, open “About Pro” and tap “Pro & Restore Purchase”. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

It takes a while after I choose a video

If the video is in iCloud Photos and not yet on your iPhone, iOS downloads it when you choose it, which can take time on a slow connection. You can cancel and choose another video instead.

The tracking lost the person partway through

Fix it in the editor: move the timeline to where it went wrong, tap “Add a ◆ here”, and drag the frame to the right place. To follow someone else, tap their face.

Can I use video shot in portrait?

VertiCut is made for footage shot in landscape. A portrait video is already tall, so there is little left to cut out of it.

Is my video ever uploaded?

No. Importing, face detection, tracking and export all happen on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

Reporting a problem

Every screen that shows an error has a “Copy details” button. It copies what we need to find the cause (device model, iOS and app version, and the last few steps with the error codes) to the clipboard. File names and video content are not included.
